Camera Path

  1. Open wide: AutoSniper turns auction listings into bid decisions.
  2. Pan left: Grays, bid refreshes, and external market evidence feed the CSV datasets.
  3. Move to the middle: normalisation, restricted universe, and curves decide what is covered.
  4. Zoom into valuation: comps, curves, repairs, and auction-price prediction produce profit bands.
  5. Move right: shared policy converts numbers into Buy, Avoid, or Review, with proxy max as the economic safety boundary.
  6. Finish bottom-right: proof pages, missed opportunities, repair pricing, and governance improve the system.

Short Voiceover

  • Buy means the current worst-case profit clears the minimum and the current bid remains within a safe auction-site proxy max. Entering that cap cannot produce an above-cap win.
  • Expected finish and historical comps indicate win likelihood and confidence; they do not block an otherwise safe proxy bid.
  • Avoid means the current bid crossed proxy max, current worst-case profit is insufficient, or a hard safety/policy condition blocks bidding.
  • Review means the system needs missing context before it can recommend action.
